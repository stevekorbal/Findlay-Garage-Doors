## Winter Maintenance Checklist for Hancock County Homeowners

Northwest Ohio winters bring freezing temperatures, heavy snowfall, and road salt that can wreak havoc on residential garage doors. Cold temperatures cause metal components to contract, harden rubber bottom weatherstripping, and thicken gear grease inside garage door openers.

A winter tune-up ensures smooth operation when temperatures drop below freezing in Findlay, Arlington, McComb, and surrounding communities.

---

## 1. Inspect and Replace Brittle Bottom Weather Seals

The rubber weatherstrip at the bottom of your garage door creates an airtight barrier against freezing drafts, blowing snow, and moisture. Over time, UV exposure and freezing ground ice cause rubber seals to crack and flatten.

- **Check for Light Leaks:** Close your garage door and turn off interior lights. Look for daylight penetrating beneath the bottom seal.
- **Test Flexibility:** Press the rubber seal with your finger. If it feels brittle like hard plastic, it needs replacement.
- **Prevent Sticking:** Apply a thin layer of silicone spray along the bottom seal to prevent ice from freezing the door to your concrete driveway.

---

## 2. Lubricate Moving Parts with Cold-Weather Synthetic Lubricant

Never use standard grease or WD-40 on garage door tracks and springs in winter! Standard grease hardens in sub-zero weather, creating sticky friction, while WD-40 is a solvent that strips protective factory oils.

Instead, apply a dedicated high-grade **silicone or lithium-based garage door lubricant** to:
- Steel rollers and ball bearings
- Torsion and extension springs
- Metal hinges and pivot points
- Lift cables and bearing plates

Proper lubrication reduces mechanical strain on your opener motor by up to **30%**.

---

## 3. Test Safety Reversal Features & Photo Eye Sensors

Snow accumulation and ice buildup near the garage threshold can obscure photo-eye safety sensors.

1. **Clean the Lenses:** Wipe both photo-eye lenses with a microfiber cloth to remove dust, frost, or spiderwebs.
2. **Alignment Check:** Ensure both sensor LED indicators are glowing steadily (not flickering).
3. **2x4 Test:** Place a clean 2x4 block of wood on the driveway floor directly beneath the door path. Press the close button on your wall console. When the bottom edge contacts the wood, the door should immediately reverse direction.

---

If your garage is attached to your home or sits directly below a master bedroom, an uninsulated steel door can cause significant heat loss. Upgrading to a triple-layer polyurethane insulated door provides:
